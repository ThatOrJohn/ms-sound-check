# 0009: Shrimp season as a lean (early / normal / late), never a date

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-28

## Context
15 seasons (2012-2026). Warmer March-April water means earlier openings; typical miss ~6 days vs 8.5 for
guessing the average; some seasons miss by weeks (2025).

## Decision
Publish a seasonal lean from spring water temperature, with its date range and caveats, and score it
publicly against MDMR's actual date each year.

## Why
The signal is real but too weak and post hoc to present as a forecast date.

## Consequences
Spring 2027 is the first true out-of-sample test.
